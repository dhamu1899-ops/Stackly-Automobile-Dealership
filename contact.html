Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting, booking, and financing at Stackly Salem.

What documents do I need to bring for a test drive?
All you need is a valid Indian driving license and a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar or Voter ID). Our sales specialist will accompany you on a curated city and highway route in Salem.
Can I exchange or trade-in my current car for a new one?
Yes! We offer on-the-spot vehicle appraisals with our certified master evaluators. We inspect your car, give you a guaranteed fair market quote in under 30 minutes, and transfer the equity toward your new purchase.
Do you offer bank financing for customers outside Salem district?
Absolutely. We are authorized partners with HDFC, State Bank of India, ICICI, and Axis Bank. We process low-interest automotive loans for buyers across all districts of Tamil Nadu with rapid digital approvals.
Where is your main service and parts center located?
Our state-of-the-art diagnostic and service center is situated along the Kondalampatti NH-44 highway bypass, fully equipped with automated alignment bays, paint booths, and computerized dyno testers.

Stackly is a premier automotive experience provider. We offer transparent pricing, 150-point certified inspections, and customer-first service across Tamil Nadu.
